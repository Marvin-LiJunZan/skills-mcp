# Academic Research Skills (ARS) Suite

The comprehensive academic research engineering toolkit, supporting end-to-end paper authoring from early hypothesis development through peer-review defense.

## Core Modules & Capabilities

1. **Deep Research & Literature Intelligence** (`deep-research/`):
   - High-precision query generation, semantic paper graph expansion, citation chain tracking.
   - S2 / OpenAlex / arXiv integration with strict provenance logging.

2. **Paper Drafting & Formatting** (`academic-paper/`):
   - Academic manuscript generation across diverse conference and journal standards (Elsevier, IEEE, Springer, ACM, Nature).
   - Paragraph-level topic sentence discipline, signposting, cohesive flow.

3. **Citation & Integrity Protocol** (`academic-pipeline/`):
   - Trust-provenance and citation drift protection.
   - Zero-orphan citation verification (all in-text citations backed by valid bib items).

4. **Peer-Review Simulation** (`academic-paper-reviewer/`):
   - Meta-reviewer and specific reviewer personas (Methodology stickler, Novelty evaluator, Literature completeness auditor).

## Standard Execution Loop

```text
Phase 1: Literature Scout & Gap Definition
Phase 2: Method Blueprint & Mathematical Rigor
Phase 3: Experiment Evidence Matrix & Result Freeze
Phase 4: Manuscript Generation (Section-by-Section)
Phase 5: Citation Compliance Audit & Auto-Correction
Phase 6: Adversarial Peer Review & Rebuttal Simulation
```
