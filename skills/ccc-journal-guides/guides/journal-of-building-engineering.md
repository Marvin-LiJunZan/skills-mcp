# Journal of Building Engineering (JOBE) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Journal of Building Engineering*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 2352-7102
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1, Impact Factor: 6.4 (5-Year IF ~6.8, CiteScore ~11.2)
- **Subject Category**: Construction & Building Technology | Engineering, Civil
- **Aims & Scope**: JOBE encompasses all aspects of science and technology related to the whole life cycle of the built environment, including building materials, structural performance, energy efficiency, construction systems, maintenance, and automated visual building inspection.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy**:
  - Requires demonstrable relevance to the whole building system and building lifecycle.
  - Welcomes holistic integration of building information modeling (BIM), automated visual inspection, and defect assessment on building facades and structures.

## 3. Formatting & Elsevier Standards

- **Highlights**: 3 to 5 bullets, strictly $\le 85$ characters per bullet.
- **Abstract**: 150 – 250 words. Focus on building engineering context, testing/inspection methodology, quantitative results, and building life-cycle impact.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`).
- **Mandatory Statements**: CRediT statement, declaration of interests, and data availability statement.
