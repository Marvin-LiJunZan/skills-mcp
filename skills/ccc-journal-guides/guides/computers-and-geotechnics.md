# Computers and Geotechnics (C&G) - Author Guide & Word Formatting Rules

- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0266-352X
- **Indexing**: JCR Q1 (Top 2%), Impact Factor: ~5.3
- **Focus**: Numerical modeling, constitutive modeling, geomechanics, geotechnical digital twins, machine learning in geotechnical engineering, underground excavation, slope stability.

## Formatting & Submission Checklist for Word:
1. **Line Spacing & Font**: 1.5 line spacing, 10.5 pt or 11 pt Times New Roman. Continuous line numbering strongly recommended for initial peer-review.
2. **Highlights**: 3–5 bullet points, strictly <= 85 characters per bullet point.
3. **Citation Style**: Elsevier Vancouver (Numbered, e.g., [1,2]) or Elsevier Harvard (Author, Year). Default CSL: `computers-and-geotechnics.csl` / `elsevier-vancouver.csl`.
4. **Tables**: Standard Three-Line Tables (booktabs), 1.5pt top/bottom rules, 0.75pt midrule, zero vertical borders. Repeat header on subsequent pages.
5. **Equations**: Native MathType / OMML, right-aligned numbering `(1)`, `(2)` with Word SEQ field bookmarks.
