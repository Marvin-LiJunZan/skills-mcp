# Journal of Materials Processing Technology (JMPT) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Journal of Materials Processing Technology*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0924-0136
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1, Impact Factor: 6.3 (5-Year IF ~6.6, CiteScore ~12.8)
- **Subject Category**: Engineering, Manufacturing | Materials Science, Multidisciplinary
- **Aims & Scope**: Covers the scientific and engineering principles governing the processing of materials, including welding, joining, metal forming, additive manufacturing, machining, and casting.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy**:
  - Requires in-depth exploration of the physical mechanisms governing defect formation, thermal cycles, microstructure evolution, or process-property dynamics.
  - Inspection and quality monitoring papers must link detected weld/fabrication defects directly to the underlying manufacturing process physics (e.g., arc voltage, heat input, shielding gas, thermal cooling rate).

## 3. Formatting & Submission Guidelines

- **Highlights**: Mandatory 3 to 5 bullets, each $\le 85$ characters including spaces.
- **Abstract**: 150 – 250 words. Focus on process parameters, physical phenomena, inspection/sensing mechanism, and manufacturing quality improvements.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`).
- **Process Details**: Full reporting of welding parameters (current, voltage, travel speed, shielding gas composition, wire feed rate, base material chemical composition).
- **Mandatory Statements**: CRediT statement, declaration of interests, and data availability statement.
