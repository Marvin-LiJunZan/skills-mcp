# NDT & E International (NDTEINT) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *NDT & E International* (Independent Nondestructive Testing and Evaluation)
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0963-8695
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1, Impact Factor: 4.2 (5-Year IF ~4.6, CiteScore ~8.4)
- **Subject Category**: Materials Science, Characterization & Testing | Instruments & Instrumentation
- **Aims & Scope**: Publishes research on all aspects of nondestructive testing (NDT), nondestructive evaluation (NDE), and structural health monitoring (SHM), including ultrasonics, eddy currents, radiography, thermography, optical metrology, acoustic emission, and automated visual defect sizing.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy**:
  - Requires physical and experimental validation of NDT/NDE techniques.
  - Defect detection and characterization algorithms must demonstrate physical metrology accuracy (e.g., millimeter-level defect sizing, depth estimation, or calibrated acoustic/optical signal inversion) verified against ground truth metallographic cross-sections or certified benchmarks.

## 3. Formatting & Submission Guidelines

- **Highlights**: 3 to 5 bullets, strictly $\le 85$ characters per bullet.
- **Abstract**: 150 – 250 words. Focus on NDT sensing principle, flaw detection/sizing methodology, quantitative experimental accuracy, and physical inspection reliability.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`).
- **Physical Calibration**: Mandates explicit reporting of optical or acoustic calibration constants (e.g., mm/pixel, ultrasonic sound velocity, sensor frequency), signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), and measurement uncertainty.
- **Mandatory Statements**: CRediT statement, declaration of interests, and data availability statement.
