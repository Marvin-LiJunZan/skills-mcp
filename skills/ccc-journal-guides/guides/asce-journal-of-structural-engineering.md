# ASCE Journal of Structural Engineering (JSE) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Journal of Structural Engineering*
- **Publisher**: American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) | **ISSN**: 0733-9445
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1/Q2, Impact Factor: 3.8 (5-Year IF ~4.2)
- **Subject Category**: Engineering, Civil | Construction & Building Technology
- **Aims & Scope**: The flagship journal of structural engineering, reporting on state-of-the-art developments in structural analysis, design, behavioral modeling, connections, extreme events, and experimental mechanics.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy**:
  - The manuscript must make a substantial contribution to structural engineering practice or structural mechanics principles.
  - Computational or vision-based inspection papers must clearly substantiate how detected flaw geometries affect structural design limits, capacity curves, or code criteria.

## 3. Mandatory Formatting & ASCE Standards

- **Unnumbered Section Headings**: All headings must be unnumbered text headings.
- **Notation List**: Mandatory before references if the paper features mathematical formulations.
- **Citation Format**: Author-Date format (`(Li and Wang 2024)`).
- **Template**: `\documentclass[Journal]{ascelike-new}`.
- **Data Availability Statement**: Mandatory ASCE standardized statement.
