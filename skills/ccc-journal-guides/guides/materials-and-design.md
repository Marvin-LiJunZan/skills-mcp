# Materials & Design (MATDES) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Materials & Design*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0264-1275
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1, Impact Factor: 8.4 (5-Year IF ~8.7, CiteScore ~14.8)
- **Subject Category**: Materials Science, Multidisciplinary | Engineering, Manufacturing
- **Aims & Scope**: Focuses on the relationship between the structure, properties, and processing of materials and their design and engineering performance. Covers structural alloys, composites, cellular materials, additive manufacturing, and advanced joining/welding.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy (Crucial for Acceptance)**:
  - **Design Context is Essential**: The journal requires papers to explicitly connect material behavior, microstructures, or defect formations to an **engineering design context, design decision, or component performance**. Pure material characterization studies without clear design or performance implications are routinely **desk-rejected**.

## 3. Mandatory Front Matter & Formatting

- **Highlights**: 3 to 5 bullets, strictly $\le 85$ characters per bullet.
- **Abstract**: 150 – 250 words. Must address: material system, fabrication/joining method, inspection or property evaluation, and design significance.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`).
- **Microstructure & Defects**: When reporting welding defects or additive manufacturing flaws, provide quantitative morphology analysis (aspect ratios, hydraulic diameters, spatial distribution) and their influence on design tolerances.
- **Mandatory Statements**: CRediT statement, declaration of interests, and data availability statement.
