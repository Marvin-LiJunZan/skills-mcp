# Cement and Concrete Composites (CCC) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Cement and Concrete Composites*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0950-4230
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1 (Top 1%), Impact Factor: 10.8 (5-Year IF ~11.5, CiteScore ~19.2)
- **Subject Category**: Materials Science, Composites | Construction & Building Technology | Engineering, Civil
- **Aims & Scope**: The journal focuses on the general field of cement and concrete composites, fiber reinforcement, polymer-cement composites, reactive powder concrete, supplementary cementitious materials, waste recycling in composites, fracture mechanics, and nondestructive/automated defect evaluation.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy (Crucial for Acceptance)**:
  - **Microstructural & Mechanistic Depth**: Pure phenomenological or trial-and-error mix design testing without establishing underlying microstructural, physical, or chemical mechanisms is **consistently desk-rejected**.
  - **Composite Performance Linkage**: If an advanced visual or computational inspection technique is presented, it must be coupled with rigorous material testing or physical morphology characterization.

## 3. Mandatory Front Matter Requirements

### 3.1 Highlights (Strict 85-Character Constraint)
- 3 to 5 bullet points.
- Strictly $\le 85$ characters (including spaces and punctuation) per bullet.
- Capture material behavior, novel composite inspection, or microstructural findings.

### 3.2 Abstract
- 150 – 250 words. Continuous single paragraph.
- State material/composite context, experimental/computational testing regime, quantitative discoveries, and physical mechanisms.

### 3.3 Graphical Abstract
- Strongly recommended. $531 \times 1328$ pixels minimum, showcasing the core composite testing or inspection workflow.

## 4. Manuscript Structure & Academic Conventions

1. **Introduction**:
   - Composite material engineering challenge.
   - Critique of existing characterization and quality control methodologies.
   - Scientific gap and working hypotheses.
   - Objectives described in fluent scholarly paragraphs.
2. **Experimental Program & Material Characterization**:
   - High-purity characterization (SEM, XRD, CT, optical metrology, or sensor setup).
   - Raw material proportions, fabrication conditions, and curing protocols.
3. **Analytical & Computational Framework**:
   - Image analysis, machine learning algorithms, or transport models.
4. **Results and Mechanistic Discussion**:
   - Direct correlation between observed defects/microstructure and mechanical/durability performance.
   - Quantitative comparative benchmarking.
5. **Conclusions**:
   - Clear bullet-free summary of verified scientific insights and composite design recommendations.

## 5. Engineering Narrative & Anti-Jargon Rules (土木/建材顶刊三大铁律)

1. **概念能用普通话讲清就不造术语**：
   - 严禁硬造复合黑话（如 *gradient stiffness diagnosis*, *conflict-aware bounded allocation* 等）。
   - 正文主线统一写成直白大白话（例如：“训练前先判断各损失项对参数更新的影响，再在限定范围内确定固定权重”）。
2. **公式只服务于问题，不为了“显得高级”堆符号**：
   - 每一个公式和符号必须对应具体的材料力学机制、本构方程或可计算步骤，拒绝伪数学化堆砌。
3. **章节标题、图题与摘要必须让工程审稿人一眼看懂**：
   - 杜绝计算机式生僻抽象词，用土木与建材专家习惯的具象工程语言命名各章节与图表。
