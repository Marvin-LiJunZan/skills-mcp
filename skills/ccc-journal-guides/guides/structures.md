# Structures (IStructE / Elsevier) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Structures*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier (in partnership with The Institution of Structural Engineers, IStructE) | **ISSN**: 2352-0124
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q2, Impact Factor: 4.1 (CiteScore ~6.8)
- **Subject Category**: Engineering, Civil | Construction & Building Technology
- **Aims & Scope**: Publishes research papers on all aspects of structural engineering, bridging academic research and professional engineering practice. Topics include structural design, behavior under extreme loads, structural assessment, structural health monitoring, and connection performance.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy**:
  - Requires demonstrable relevance to practical structural design and assessment.
  - Welcomes papers combining physical experimental testing, numerical FE modeling, and automated inspection technologies for structural members and connections.

---

## 3. Mandatory Formatting & Elsevier Standards

- **Highlights**: Exactly 3 to 5 bullet points, each $\le 85$ characters including spaces.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`), cited in-text as `[1]`, `[2,3]`.
- **Structural Relevance**: The abstract and introduction must explicitly explain how the research impacts structural engineering practice, structural reliability, or standard compliance (Eurocodes, AISC, BS standards).
- **Mandatory Statements**: CRediT authorship statement, declaration of interests, and data availability statement.
