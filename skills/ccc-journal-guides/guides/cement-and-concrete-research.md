# Cement and Concrete Research (CCR) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Cement and Concrete Research*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0008-8846
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1 (Top 1%), Impact Factor: 11.4 (5-Year IF ~12.2, CiteScore ~20.5)
- **Subject Category**: Materials Science, Multidisciplinary | Construction & Building Technology
- **Aims & Scope**: The flagship and most prestigious journal in the science of cement, cementitious materials, and concrete. Focuses on fundamental chemical, physical, and thermodynamic mechanisms governing cement hydration, pore structure, chemical durability, degradation, and advanced characterization.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy (Crucial for Acceptance)**:
  - **Fundamental Science Over Engineering Application**: CCR demands **fundamental scientific novelty**. Applied papers testing routine formulas, standard civil engineering applications, or standard deep learning on surface crack images without deep material physics or chemical mechanics will be **immediately rejected by the Editor-in-Chief**.
  - **Highest Standards of Rigor**: High reproducibility, quantitative phase analysis (e.g., Rietveld XRD, nano-CT, thermodynamics modeling), and rigorous statistical validation.

## 3. Mandatory Front Matter & Formatting

- **Highlights**: Mandatory 3 to 5 bullets, each strictly $\le 85$ characters.
- **Abstract**: 150 – 250 words. Focus on chemical/physical mechanism, thermodynamic basis, or fundamental transport phenomenon.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`).
- **Scale Bars**: All micrographs, CT cross-sections, or morphology images must include high-contrast, calibrated physical scale bars and acquisition parameters.
- **Mandatory Disclosures**: CRediT authorship statement, data availability statement, declaration of interests.
