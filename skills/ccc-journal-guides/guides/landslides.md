# Landslides - Journal of the International Consortium on Landslides - Author Guide & Formatting Rules

- **Publisher**: Springer | **ISSN**: 1612-510X
- **Indexing**: JCR Q1, Impact Factor: ~6.7
- **Focus**: Landslide mechanisms, prediction, hazard assessment, remote sensing/SAR monitoring, early warning systems, slope reinforcement, digital twins for geotechnical risk.

## Formatting & Submission Checklist for Word:
1. **Line Spacing & Font**: 1.5 or double line spacing, Times New Roman 11 pt or 12 pt, wide margins (2.5 cm / 1 inch).
2. **Citation Style**: Springer Basic Author-Date (e.g., Varnes 1978; Cruden and Varnes 1996). Default CSL: `landslides.csl` / `springer-basic-author-date.csl`.
3. **Figures & Multi-panel**: High-contrast geological/field photos and numerical contour maps. Subfigures aligned in 1x2 or 2x2 borderless grid with distinct `(a)`, `(b)` labels. Minimum 300-600 DPI.
4. **Tables**: Academic Three-Line Tables (no vertical grids).
5. **Mathematical Notations**: MathType equations, italicized scalar variables ($c$, $\phi$, $\gamma$, $F_s$).
