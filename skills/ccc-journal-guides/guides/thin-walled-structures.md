# Thin-Walled Structures (TWST) - Guide for Authors & Adaptation Rules

## 1. Journal Profile & Editorial Philosophy

- **Official Name**: *Thin-Walled Structures*
- **Publisher**: Elsevier | **ISSN**: 0263-8231
- **Indexing & Metrics**: JCR Q1, Impact Factor: 6.4 (5-Year IF ~6.6)
- **Subject Category**: Engineering, Civil | Mechanics | Materials Science, Characterization & Testing
- **Aims & Scope**: Dedicated to the publication of papers in the fields of thin-walled structural members, steel structures, cold-formed sections, plates, shells, tubular joints, welded connections, and additive manufactured cellular structures.
- **Core Editorial Philosophy**:
  - Focuses on structural stability, buckling, fatigue, welded connection integrity, and experimental/numerical validation of thin-walled and lightweight structural systems.
  - Papers focusing on welded seam inspection or defect detection must connect defect metrology to residual structural capacity, stress concentrations, or joint fatigue behavior.

## 3. Mandatory Front Matter & Formatting

- **Highlights**: 3 to 5 bullets, strictly $\le 85$ characters per bullet.
- **Abstract**: 150 – 250 words. Focus on structural configuration, testing/inspection methodology, quantitative defect/stress results, and structural performance takeaways.
- **Reference Style**: Elsevier Numbered format (`elsarticle-num`) or author-date during initial review ("Your Paper Your Way").
- **Figure Quality**: High-resolution diagrams illustrating failure modes, FEA stress contours, or optical inspection results ($\ge 300\text{ dpi}$ halftones, $\ge 1000\text{ dpi}$ line art).
- **Mandatory Statements**: CRediT statement, declaration of interests, and data availability statement.
