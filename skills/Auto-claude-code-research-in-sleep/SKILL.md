# Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep (ARIS Framework)

Autonomous overnight machine learning research and adversarial multi-model auto-review loops for Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity.

---

## Capabilities & Architecture

1. **Idea Discovery & Experiment Bridge**:
   - Converts high-level research questions into verifiable numerical hypotheses and PyTorch experiment runs.
2. **Cross-Model Adversarial Auto-Review Loops**:
   - Multi-agent peer review pitting Claude, Gemini, and GPT against each other to ruthlessly identify experimental gaps, baseline flaws, and overclaimed conclusions.
3. **Manuscript & Rebuttal Generation**:
   - Full publication drafting, LaTeX packaging, reviewer point-by-point rebuttal drafting.

The canonical implementation and sub-modules are located in `skills/auto-research-in-sleep/`.
