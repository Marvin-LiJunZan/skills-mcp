# Human Writing (活人感写作 / 去 AI 味)

Human Writing systematically purges LLM-generated prose of its characteristic "AI aroma", replacing formulaic mechanical phrasing with the dynamic rhythm, precise verbs, and nuanced pacing of experienced human researchers.

---

## 1. The Core AI Hallmarks to Eliminate

| Typical AI Pattern | Why It Feels Artificial | Authentic Human Academic Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| *"Delve into", "Tapestry", "Testament to", "Beacon", "Pivotal role"* | Overused AI filler tropes; zero informational value | Direct, concrete action verbs: *analyze, measure, decouple, bound, observe* |
| *"It is worth noting that..." / "It is crucial to highlight..."* | Defensive throat-clearing | State the fact directly: *"Crucially, ... "* or omit entirely. |
| Monotonous sentence lengths (all 22–26 words) | Metronomic cadence characteristic of transformers | Alternate between punchy short statements (8 words) and complex syntactical arguments (35 words). |
| Perfect symmetric triads (*"X, Y, and Z"*) in every paragraph | Rigid artificial balance | Break the symmetry; emphasize the dominant factor and subordinate minor ones. |
| Incessant passive voice (*"It was observed that the load was increased by..."*) | Distant, robotic impersonality | Active voice: *"Increasing the axial load accelerated crack propagation..."* |

---

## 2. The 4 Humanizing Principles

1. **Information Density First**: Cut adjectives that don't quantify. If a word can be removed without altering the mathematical meaning, delete it.
2. **Dynamic Rhythm (Music of the Sentence)**:
   > *"This sentence has five words. Here are five more words. Five-word sentences are okay. No one talks like this."*
   > *Mix short sentences for impact. Then, when explaining an intricate mathematical coupling or multi-stage physical boundary phenomenon, let the subordinate clauses unfold with deliberate precision.*
3. **Conversational Academic Grounding**: Write like an expert presenting across a seminar table, not like a compliance manual.
4. **Concrete Over Abstract**: Replace *"exhibited a remarkable enhancement in resilience"* with *"absorbed $24\%$ more fracture energy before first-crack initiation"*.

---

## 3. Humanizing Self-Audit Checklist

Before approving any paper section, run this check:
- [ ] No occurrences of: `delve`, `tapestry`, `realm`, `cornerstone`, `underscore the importance of`, `testament`.
- [ ] At least two sentences shorter than 10 words per page to anchor core conclusions.
- [ ] Every paragraph begins with a strong, definitive assertion rather than a vague topic cushion.
